## Main result and contributions (agreed direction, 2026-09-21)

**Main result (Daniel):** latent-endpoint circuits are explained by tri-amp
weighted circuits of 10^2-10^3 upstream latents with high evaluation scores.
Literature-safe wording (3 novelty audits): do NOT claim first feature-endpoint
circuits (He 2402.12201, Ge 2405.13868, Dunefsky 2406.11944, CircuitLens
2510.14936, concept-targeted attribution 2608.27510) or first learned masks over
SAE latents (Caples et al., LessWrong Jan 2025 — the gate-only mask is our FAILING
baseline, 3/21 vs 17/21). Claim the validated CONJUNCTION:

> For arbitrary SAE latents, a context-reusable weighted circuit of 10^2-10^3
> upstream latents across attention, MLP and residual sites is SUFFICIENT for the
> latent under three ablation conventions and NECESSARY for it, under free
> re-execution of the real model, held-out, against capacity-matched random
> circuits, across ~15k latents per model in three models. ("To our knowledge";
> Top-K stated as scope; public models = replication, not a contribution.)

Size half already holds: 15,046 TuringLLM circuits, median 321 members (p10 101,
p90 593, max 2,311), 89.8% within 10^2-10^3, 98-99.9% from L3 up; median alpha
1.05. NB size is partly set by lambda: claim "circuits of this size PASS", not
"this is the mechanism's size". (D) Whether "and can DRIVE it where silent" joins
the headline depends on the Phase 1 numbers (drive 0.93 on Pythia, collapses with
depth on GPT-2).

**Method contribution:** weighted circuits — fitted per-member gains as part of
the circuit; joint triple-floor training; fitted-amplitude nulls. The citable
finding is the TRIAD: same members alpha=1 -> ~0.09; fitted alpha -> ~0.95; random
members with fitted alpha never pass. Closest precedent for gains: Gao et al.
2511.13653 App. F.2 (hand rescaling of two scalars, framed as a caution: "can
hide superposition"); also Stoehr 2410.04962, Li & Janson 2409.09951.

**Secondary contributions, ranked by novelty x interest (literature audit):**
1. Evaluation protocol — training floor decides membership (Jaccard 0.13 vs
   0.54); dense vs Top-K fill disagree up to 29x; free execution diverges on
   uncapped JumpReLU, not Top-K, and the fix. (Extends Miller 2407.08734, MIB
   2504.13151, Dead Salmons 2512.18792, 2606.18322.)
2. Inhibitors and role-aware necessity — role-blind necessity goes NEGATIVE;
   role-aware restores it; both-direction causal test; amplitudes as brakes.
3. Scale + circuit graph — ~15k validated circuits per model; hubs (address the
   density/interference confound; cite Turner-Wu-Batson Aug 2026, Kendiukhov).
4. Negative contexts + DRIVE — retrieved silent-but-near contexts as baseline,
   floor and drive arena; drive != restoration. "Random beats hard negatives" =
   caution only.
5. Non-uniqueness — short honesty section; the precision lower bound (~16%
   members exchanged by float rounding) is the part others lack. Cite
   2606.06267, 2605.12671, 2606.16920, 2602.22968, Meloux 2502.20914.
Plus the behaviour-endpoint boundary as a thesis-supporting result (re-run).
